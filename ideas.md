# DSE Physics Revision Website — Design Ideas

## Approach A: Scientific Blueprint
- **Design Movement**: Technical Blueprint / Engineering Schematic
- **Core Principles**: Grid-based precision, monochrome with accent highlights, data-dense but readable
- **Color Philosophy**: Dark navy (#0a0f1e) background with electric blue (#00d4ff) accents; white text for readability
- **Layout Paradigm**: Left sidebar navigation + main content area; chapter cards arranged in asymmetric masonry
- **Signature Elements**: Dotted grid background, glowing formula boxes, circuit-line dividers
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Hover reveals Chinese annotations; accordion-style formula expansion
- **Animation**: Fade-in with slight translateY on section load; formula boxes pulse on hover
- **Typography**: "Space Grotesk" for headings (technical feel), "IBM Plex Mono" for formulas, "Noto Sans TC" for Chinese annotations
- **Probability**: 0.07

## Approach B: Academic Notebook
- **Design Movement**: Clean Academic / Scientific Journal
- **Core Principles**: Off-white paper texture, ink-like typography, margin annotations, structured hierarchy
- **Color Philosophy**: Warm cream (#faf8f3) background with deep charcoal (#1a1a2e) text; amber (#f59e0b) for highlights and Chinese notes
- **Layout Paradigm**: Single-column scrollable "notebook" with sticky left chapter index; content feels like handwritten notes
- **Signature Elements**: Ruled line backgrounds, sticky-note style Chinese annotations, hand-drawn style diagram borders
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Click to expand sections; Chinese annotations appear as margin notes
- **Animation**: Page-turn effect on chapter change; subtle ink-draw animation for diagrams
- **Typography**: "Playfair Display" for chapter titles, "Source Serif 4" for body, "Noto Serif TC" for Chinese
- **Probability**: 0.06

## Approach C: Dark Physics Lab
- **Design Movement**: Modern Dark Dashboard / Physics Lab Interface
- **Core Principles**: Deep dark background, neon accent colors per topic, card-based modular layout, high contrast
- **Color Philosophy**: Near-black (#0d1117) base; each topic gets a distinct neon accent (optics=cyan, mechanics=orange, waves=purple, heat=red); creates visual topic differentiation
- **Layout Paradigm**: Top navigation bar + topic cards grid on homepage; each topic page uses a two-column layout (concept left, formulas/diagrams right)
- **Signature Elements**: Glowing topic cards, color-coded formula pills, animated wave/ray diagrams using CSS
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth page transitions; formula cards flip to show Chinese explanation; progress indicator
- **Animation**: Card entrance with stagger; formula highlight on hover; subtle particle background on hero
- **Typography**: "Syne" for headings (bold, geometric), "DM Sans" for body, "Noto Sans TC" for Chinese annotations
- **Probability**: 0.09

---

**Selected Approach: C — Dark Physics Lab**

Rationale: The dark lab aesthetic creates an immersive study environment that reduces eye strain during long revision sessions. The color-coded topic system helps students quickly navigate between subjects, and the high contrast makes formulas and key concepts stand out clearly.
